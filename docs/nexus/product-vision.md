# NEXUS Product Vision

NEXUS is a Microsoft 365-native enterprise companion platform. Outlook is the first daily user interface, while the browser application is the administration and governance layer.

## Primary value proposition

Companies should be able to centralise approved knowledge, wording, procedures, contact directories, escalation rules, and AI behaviour so employees across every office respond consistently and safely.

## Core users

- HQ administrators maintaining company knowledge and rules.
- Office managers supervising response quality and escalation handling.
- Employees working from Outlook who need fast, approved replies.
- Executives reviewing response times, risk, escalations, and activity.

## Core product surfaces

1. Browser admin portal.
2. Outlook task pane add-in.
3. Microsoft Graph integration layer.
4. AI and rules engine.
5. Analytics and audit dashboard.

## MVP outcomes

The first production milestone should prove that a user can open an email in Outlook, view a summary, receive a company-approved suggested reply, see relevant templates and knowledge, and understand whether the message needs escalation.

## Long-term direction

NEXUS should become the operational intelligence layer for Microsoft 365, with future support for Teams notifications, SharePoint knowledge indexing, approval workflows, response-time analytics, and Microsoft AppSource distribution.
